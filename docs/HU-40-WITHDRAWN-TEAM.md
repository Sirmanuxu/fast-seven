# HU-40 - Withdrawn team

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Retire a team during a running tournament; convert its resolved pending matches to configured W.O. results and advance the opponent in elimination.
- [x] Automatically convert later pending matches when their references resolve to a withdrawn team.
- [x] Leave finished matches and player records unchanged.
- [x] Require a team in an in-progress match to be resolved first; allow its W.O. resolution and remove its live events.
- [x] Leave a match between two withdrawn teams pending for manual direct-result resolution; require the sudden-death winner for a tied elimination result.
- [x] Mark withdrawn teams in the standings and retain W.O. score and table-point accounting.
- [x] Reinstate a team and reset only its withdrawal-generated W.O. matches to `PENDING`.
- [x] Do not regenerate or recalculate group membership; warn that groups may be unbalanced.
- [x] Persist player, team, match, event, and backup changes atomically.
- [x] Cover the HU-40 reference case and withdrawal edge cases with tests.

## Manual verification

1. Run `npm start`, create a tournament with at least four teams, generate its fixture, and start it.
2. In **Equipos**, choose «Retirar equipo» on a team with pending matches. Confirm the warning and verify its scheduled opponents receive the configured score, while finished matches stay unchanged.
3. Check **Posiciones**: the team is labeled «Retirado», its W.O. losses count using the configured walkover-loss points, and the group composition remains unchanged.
4. Open **Mesa** and start a match involving a team you plan to retire. Choose W.O.; confirm any live events will be removed. Verify the match finishes and the team can then be retired.
5. In a groups-plus-knockout tournament, withdraw a team that could qualify. Once the group results resolve the bracket slot, verify the newly playable match becomes a W.O. and its opponent advances.
6. Withdraw both teams in one pending match. Verify no winner is assigned automatically, the W.O. action is unavailable, and the match can be resolved through direct score / sudden death.
7. Reincorporate a withdrawn team. Verify only W.O. results generated by its withdrawal return to `PENDING`; manually entered W.O. results remain finished.

## Decisions and notes

- Automatic W.O. results carry optional `walkoverOrigin` metadata with the withdrawn team ID. This makes reinstatement precise without changing Dexie indexes or requiring a schema migration.
- A match between two withdrawn teams remains pending for manual resolution, following HU-37's rule that double W.O. has no automatic winner. This conservative interpretation was used because clarification was unavailable.
- A team cannot be withdrawn while it has a match in progress. The match can first be finalized normally or by W.O.; the W.O. transaction removes its events.
- Newly resolvable matches against withdrawn teams are completed as W.O. after each result, so unresolved bracket references do not stall the tournament.
- Withdrawal and reinstatement transactions create a pre-change snapshot backup and leave player records and group membership unchanged.
